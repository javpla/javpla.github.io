The game pauses while this menu is open.

Move — ← / →: run on the sand. A running jump keeps your takeoff momentum. You cannot steer in the air. Jumping into the net is a foul.

Touch — ↑: press as the ball reaches you. No hold is needed. Pass, set and attack are automatic. An accepted header waits for head contact; pressing too early still misses. Timing quality comes from your press.

Serve — ↑: press when the serve cue is a dot. Green is perfect, yellow is good, red is bad.

Jump — ↓: tap for a hop with your head just below the net top. Hold and release at the compact ring's dot for a high jump. Press Up separately to touch.

Jump cue: green predicts a reachable ball at your apex with your current takeoff momentum. Amber controls height only.

Aim — ← / → at contact: toward the net sends the ball farther; away sends it shorter. Aim still works in the air, without steering the player.

Attack: perfect passes and sets stay close to the net. Late foot sets are high but imprecise. Two perfect setup touches, a perfect full jump and a timed apex header give the hardest attack.

Settings: Esc or Menu pauses the game. L toggles the landing marker. D toggles debug text. R restarts. A keyboard is required.
